WRITER'S LANE
Brennan's Weblog
https://brennan.day
On the corner of a road, there's a rather simple house accented with rainbows. You can see coffee mugs and scattered books of poetry, someone types between chess games on their laptop in thewindow. Behind them, shelves overflow with reference manuals for blog themes they've built for the open web. They are writing like they are running out of time. They have published nine books without permission from anyone. 500,000 words and counting, all tagged and cross-referenced across a dozen webrings.
