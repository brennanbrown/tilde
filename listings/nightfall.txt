Brennan's House on the Shore
============================

Welcome to my little corner of Nightfall City! I'm Brennan 
(he/they), a Queer Métis author and web developer based in 
Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

About Me
--------

I wear many hats: founder of Berry House 
(https://berryhouse.ca), a values-driven web studio; 
independent literary journalist; and founder of Write Club
(https://writeclub.netlify.app), a creative collective for writers. 
I'm passionate about digital autonomy, accessibility, and 
building a more independent web.

Quick Facts
-----------

- Location: Calgary, Alberta, Canada (Treaty 7 territory)
- Age: 30
- Education: BA English (Honours) with minors in 
  Creative Writing & Religious Studies
- Writing: Published 9 books, 300+ articles since 2015
- Focus: Creative nonfiction, lyric essays, digital 
  culture, accessibility

What I Do
---------

Writing
-------
I primarily write creative nonfiction and lyric essays on 
topics like writing craft, literary criticism, digital 
minimalism, and the independent web. Most of my 
longform work lives at blog.brennanbrown.ca
(https://blog.brennanbrown.ca).

Web Development
---------------
I build fast, accessible, content-first websites using 
static-site tooling. I care about performance, 
accessibility (A11y-first), and helping people own their 
digital presence.
