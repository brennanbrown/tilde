PGP

Plain-text email is a feature, not a limitation: it reads in every client on every device, archives well, and quotes cleanly. PGP adds encryption and verification without changing that. To send me encrypted mail, import the key below, check that the fingerprint matches, then encrypt to mail@brennanbrown.ca. New to PGP? The FSF's Email Self-Defense is a good start, and useplaintext.email makes the case for plain text.
