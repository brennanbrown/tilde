REMARKS ON BOARD GENAWAABOONAGAK
Mission Day 4391, Book V of the continuous log
this entry: E. Brown-Okimakan, Master and Keeper

Ascension   07h 39m 25s     Declination    +19 deg 11'
Distance    2.87 ly         Fix            RS001 two-star
Solar flux  344 W/m2        Sail           all plain, drawing
Watchers afield: twelve

AM:  Hailed the net at 0600. My tender legs still
     adjusting, Watcher-12 first and loudest, eager
     as a new hand. None brought aught to alarm;
     the black sea we watch is calm. Watcher-04 kept
     her silence, tallying four-and-forty days.

     Tended the three still racked at 0900. The
     solar-glass reads fair, if fair is the
     word for it: flux steady, wind aft of the
     beam, and the sail drawing. Worked a point
     to starboard, I know the ship answers a
     sheet quicker than her new captain.

PM:  Took the noon fix myself; holding against the
     chart. Stood the middle watch, third night
     running. Mémère watch once, I carry the toll
     unsure. Her cabin stands as she left it; I keep
     the day-room. The watchers say "captain"
     like a question. Let them ask a while.

     I write small, I know. Her storytime and
     hand filled these columns; mine's just at
     the margin. The book teaches the hand, she
     used to say. I am trying to let it.

     God keep the ship, and all who watch.
