REMARKS ON BOARD GENAWAABOONAGAK
Mission Day 4393, Book V of the continuous log
this entry: E. Brown-Okimakan, Master and Keeper

Ascension   07h 39m 28s     Declination    +19 deg 10'
Distance    2.87 ly         Fix            RS001 two-star
Solar flux  655 W/m2        Sail           reefed double, rode
Watchers afield: twelve

AM:  Watcher-09 reported a gust before it reached
     us, flux climbing and some hard weather on her
     windward side. The flare missed us by a wide
     berth. Close enough to reef the main double
     and strike the vanes, far enough that we took
     only the spray. The chain worked as drawn: a
     bird sunward saw it first and called home
     before it arrived. All twelve hail true.
     Watcher-04 keeps silence at six-and-forty
     days.

PM:  In the wash of it the relay gave us a voice.
     Weak signal, source tag corrupt, no fix. Hm, a
     log rather than a letter, meant for no one and
     for everyone. I read only fragments: SHATTERED,
     SCATTERED, and I AND I AND I, repeated until
     the words stop meaning. A siren, or a ghost,
     something lingers there in the impossible long
     dark.

     I have no answer to send, for the address was
     drowned and garbled. Not enough left for me.
     Copied the piece into the back of the book
     and logged it under weather, for want of a
     better column. I'd summon the spirit again
     despite the terror, just to have a penmate.

     God keep the ship, and all who watch.
